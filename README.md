## 🎯 Problem Statement
*Describe the specific pain point or challenge here (Keep it under 3 lines).*
* **Context:** EVERYONE WANTS COMFORT AT THEIR DOORSTEP WHEN TALKING ABOUT THIS OUR APP HELPS IN KEEPING IT THAT WAY BY HELPING THEM FIND THE BEST SOLUTION TO REPAIR ANYTHING ACCORDING TO IT.
* **Impact:** THIS WILL HAVE A GREAT IMPACT AND IF A USER HAS ONCE JOINED THIS APP HE WILL NOT BE ABLE TO FIND ANY OTHER AS THIS AS OF ADDING EVERYTHING CUSTOMISED TO CUSTOMER NEEDS.

## 💡 Solution
*Describe your approach and how it solves the problem.*
1.  **Key Point 1:** CUSTOMISED TALKING TO THE PERSON AND UNDERSTANDING HIS PROBLEMS.
2.  **Key Point 2:** USING AI TO SEE SHOPKEEPER PREVIOUS PRICES TO GIVE THE USER AND SHOPKEEPER THE PERFECT ONE.
3.  **Key Point 3:** OUR APP LOCATE THE NEAREST DELIVERY PARTNERS TO CUSTOMER AND ASSIGN IT.
